Bar

One full-width bar per monitor: a plain 34 px rectangle flush with the top edge and both sides, with no margins, rounded corners or shadow. Workspaces sit on the left and status and clocks on the right; the middle stays empty. Shown here at 1136 px wide: a wider monitor only adds empty middle.

Left to right: workspaces 1–9, each showing the icons of its windows, and the current workspace's layout symbol (|M| = three-column). Right: third-party tray icons (StatusNotifierItem), then the built-in status icons (Bluetooth, network, volume, battery, notifications, session), then the clocks. The zone clocks carry short place labels (SF, NYC, LON), so nothing changes at DST; the clocks popover adds each zone’s current abbreviation (PDT/PST, EDT/EST, BST/GMT). The local clock swaps its label for the date.

Status states

screen_shareSharingmic
Screen shared / mic live. The red pill shows while a PipeWire screencast stream has a consumer; during a screen or region share, popups are held until it ends.
notifications_paused
Do not disturb. The icon changes; held popups go to the history.
coffee
Keeping awake: on while you asked for it, or while the mic is live. It turns itself off after 2 h.
battery_1_bar9%
Battery low: red below 15%, with a critical notification at 7%.
wifi_off
Offline. A VPN adds a small lock to the network icon.
